About Me

I'm Christoffer Aidan A. Domantay and I'm a 1st Year BSIT Multimedia Arts & Animation student of Universidad De Dagupan. I used to live in Baguio City for the entirity of my adolescent years, but I moved back here to my hometown of Dagupan City so that me and my family would continue living with each other (Baguio rent prices are so pricey ugh).

I have a particular set of skills that would make me a perfect example of what a multimedia artist can do. I like to interact with a lot of forms of ditial media and I like to make a lot of personal projects with it passionately.

As a Multimedia Arts student, I hope to hone my skills so that I could make it into this unforgiving job market while improving the quality of my personal projects.

Projects

Coast Pyter

Coast Pyter is 2D fighting game that is run with Pygames. It is a two player game where each controls two different Sora and fight til one of them defeats the othe for two rounds. The assets of the game is heavily inspired by the beloved JRPG, Kingdom Hearts, while the mechanics of the game is mainly inspired by an indie fighting game, Footsies. You can visit the game site here to check more about it.

Java Mystery Game

This Java game was made as a final project in the Programming 1 subject in my 1st year of college. It uses the JOptionPane as its GUI and its inspired by the mystery visual novel style game called "Phoenix Wright: Ace Attorney". This is the first game that I have ever made and I cringe everytime I think about it because I used a for loop instead of a while loop for the main game loop.

Suntukan sa Yangbew

This video is a passion project that I made this year where I showcased my love for the genre. This video takes place in the mountain of Yangbew located in La Trinidad, Benguet where me and my friend stumble upon each other and started duking it out. This video is actually my second martial arts video with the same premise and characters so there's a bit of lore there. I am currently quite proud of how this video turned out because of how I improved my choreography, directing, editing, and storyboarding skills. My only regret was that I wish that I could make longer choreography sequences.
